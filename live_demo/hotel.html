The neighbourhood

Everything worth walking to, within nine hundred seconds

The old town unfolds exactly as a hotel lobby should not — crooked, loud, fragrant. Here is what guests find outside our door.

  • Playa de la Sala — sand & chiringuitos 3 min walk
  • Mercado Viejo — market & tapas bars 6 min walk
  • Castillo de Santa Ana — viewpoint 9 min walk
  • Casco Antiguo — cathedral & galleries 4 min walk
  • Botanical promenade — sunrise laps 2 min walk
